- **Favicon and touch icon** (`public/favicon.ico`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png`): the business's existing sunburst "FWB BOWL EST '95" badge, from their current favicon. Same terms as above.

## Artwork

Everything else is original to this project, drawn in SVG and CSS for this site: the perspective lane art with the FWB BOWL masking panels, starburst badges, logo-panel strips and icon tiles, ticket stubs, score-sheet price cards, the party illustration and the icons. Free to use for this site.

The social share image (`public/og-image.jpg`) is generated by `scripts/make-og.mjs` from the logo and the fonts above.

## Not used

- **No photos or graphics from the current fwbbowl.com.** That site says its images can't be republished. Its screenshots and flyers were only used as reference to read prices, and they're kept out of this repo (see `reference/README.md`).
- **No stock photos.** Photo spots are marked for the owner's own pictures.
